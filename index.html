The NaomiCrowns Promise

Simple, transparent policies designed with you in mind.

About NaomiCrowns

  • Based in JHB & Polokwane, we source and sell our own premium wigs and frontals.
  • Our mission: Make you look and feel like royalty without breaking the bank.
  • Every "crown" is carefully inspected and styled before it leaves our hands.
  • Loved by over 500+ ladies across South Africa.

Shipping & Delivery

  • Processing: 1 to 2 days (weekdays and weekends) before dispatch.
  • Delivery: Takes 2 to 5 business working days or 5 to 12 business working days depending on the type of product.
  • Partners: We use Paxi (PEP) and PostNet to ship our products to you.
  • Weekends: No shipments on weekends or public holidays unless negotiated 5 days before.
  • Late Delivery: If it takes more than 10 working days, you get R100 off your next order.

Returns & Exchanges

  • No Refunds: Due to hygiene, we don't offer cash refunds.
  • Exchanges: Allowed within 5 days of receiving your order on certain products.
  • Condition: Lace must NOT be cut, trimmed, or altered. Wig must be unworn.
  • Costs: Customers cover the return shipping costs.
  • Defective? Notify us within 7 days for a replacement or store credit.

Payments & Orders

  • 100% Upfront: Full payment required to secure your order.
  • Secure Checkout: Safe and encrypted payment gateways.
  • Price Lock: Prices are locked at checkout. Later sales don't apply.
  • Out of Stock? We'll notify you immediately and help source it.
